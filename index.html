About

Tzofi Klinghoffer is a PhD candidate at the MIT Media Lab, advised by Prof. Ramesh Raskar. He works at the intersection of artificial intelligence, computer vision, and sensing, with expertise in single-photon vision. His research asks how machines can discover new ways to sense the world, through two complementary directions:

  • Learning from sensors: what information can measurements reveal? (physics-based learning, sensor foundation models)
  • Learning for sensors: what should we measure, and how? (task-driven design, data-driven simulation)

The two directions work as a loop. Learning for sensors proposes candidate sensing systems and predicts what they would measure. Learning from sensors interprets those measurements and scores them on the task, which guides the next design. The aim is to discover new ways of sensing the world, with sensing, perception, and action co-designed around a downstream task.

During his PhD, Tzofi has collaborated with Meta Reality Labs and NVIDIA Research, and was part of the founding team at Code Metal. Previously, he was a software engineer in Alexa AI at Amazon and an AI researcher at MIT Lincoln Laboratory. Tzofi is a DoD NDSEG Fellow, Qualcomm Innovation Fellow, and Draper Scholar. He received his SM from MIT and his BS in Computer Science, summa cum laude, from The University of Alabama.

Tzofi is on the faculty job market this cycle. You can find his research statement here.
