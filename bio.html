Bio

Tzofi Klinghoffer is a final-year Ph.D. candidate in the MIT Media Lab, working with Ramesh Raskar. His research lies at the intersection of sensing, 3D vision/rendering, and machine learning. His goal is to enable new vision and sensing capabilities by combining emerging sensors with machine learning. Towards that goal, his work focuses on learning hidden information from sensors, rendering- and data-driven methods for modeling 3D worlds, and discovering how and what should be sensed. His long term goal is to bring these together to enable co-design of sensing, perception, and control. His work has been published at top vision, graphics, and imaging venues, such as CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, ICCP, and SIGGRAPH Asia, and was recognized with a Best Paper Award Finalist nomination at CVPR 2024. He is a Draper Scholar, Qualcomm Innovation Fellow, and DoD NDSEG Fellow.
